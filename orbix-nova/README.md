# ORBIX NOVA: Evolution of Space Telescopes

A browser game for ages 12-18 that teaches how space telescopes evolved, from early orbital observatories to Hubble-class and Webb-class telescopes.

## How to play
1. Drag a frame, optics, power and instruments into the assembly cradle.
2. Launch through a 3D atmosphere of clouds, NO2 smog and aerosols.
3. Take a blurry first photo in orbit by keeping the reticle on target.
4. Clean the image in the lab (sharpening, noise filter, wavelength calibration, gas filters) to earn grant money.
5. Collect every photo in the gallery. Design mistakes show up in flight, followed by a report explaining the cause.

## Tools used
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas 2D
- three.js r128 (MIT licence) loaded from cdnjs
- Artwork and textures are generated in code; no external images
